# Die Suppe: 6 prompts of 10 seconds for Gemini (Veo)

Each clip holds TWO sentences of one person with a silent listening pause between them. Afterwards the clips are cut and the two people are interleaved into the phone call.
Settings: vertical 9:16, **10 seconds**, the character picture attached as the reference (**chefin** for clips 1, 3, 5; **koch** for clips 2, 4, 6), no music, no text on the screen.
Limit per round: 4 clips. Do clips 1-4 first (Batch 1), the rest (clips 5-6) later (Batch 2). Send me the clips as they are made; the order does not matter, the file name should have the clip number.

## Clip 1 (Batch 1) — attach: chefin

A 2D cartoon in exactly the same style, face, hair and clothes as the attached picture. Fixed camera, medium shot from the knees up, the woman in a bright modern office hallway with peach walls and ceiling lights, holding a smartphone to her ear for the whole clip. For the first 3 seconds she says in German: "Wo ist die Suppe?" (she looks impatient with one raised eyebrow). Then, for about 3 seconds, she stays silent with the mouth closed and she listens to the answer, her expression turning from impatient to annoyed. In the last 4 seconds she says in German: "Gleich? Der Gast wartet seit einer Stunde!" (she is furious, angry frown, shouting). Only the room sound, no music.
