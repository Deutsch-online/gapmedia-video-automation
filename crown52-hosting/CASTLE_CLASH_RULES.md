# Castle Clash LIVE 80

The actual game separates board art from live HUD, player tokens, HP, actions, missiles, shields, support ribbons, results and host controls. The generated board has no baked player identities, scores or UI. Island Rescue remains removed and must not be reinstated.

First comment 1/2/3 joins a balanced team and submits Attack/Defend/Help in one step. Persian/Arabic/Latin digits, keycaps, A/D/H and Persian action words work. Optional RED/BLUE/GREEN or Persian colour words select a team. An active player's team change waits until the next round. One effective order per 8-second order window; at most three revisions with 900ms between them. A comment during the 2-second clash queues for the next order window.

A normal round contains six 10-second turns, followed by 8 seconds of results. Host can start/resume, pause, skip, reset, switch auto restart, or choose 60/90/120-second future rounds. Pausing shifts authoritative deadlines. Choices, consequences and scores are computed only on the server.

Team strength uses the fraction of active orders, not total crowd size. Attack pressure 18*A/N targets a leading opponent, with rotating tie targeting. Defence absorbs 14*D/N and bounded cheer help. Support restores up to12 actor HP and up to8/N castle HP; when nothing needs healing it fortifies the current clash. Actual blocked/healed/effective damage drive role awards. No award message claims an event that did not occur.

Capture progress is credited from effective damage, guarded territory and support. It is quantized to0.1 for coherent displayed ties. A unique leader holds the central crown and gains3 progress per turn; tied leaders do not receive an arbitrary crown. A breached castle resets to70 HP and loses8 capture points, allowing a comeback. Each round resets game XP and team progress, while actual liker/gifter archive remains unchanged.
