# Bubble lifecycle, stages and narrator — version65

- Lower arena: up to12 drifting portrait bubbles and3 active podium seats. A new arrival starts tiny; after30 seconds without a reported departure it becomes a small settled bubble. A silent newcomer disappears at60 seconds. Presence is inferred from available events: TikTok does not report every departure.
- Every actual like, finalised gift or comment renews a60-second active window. At60 seconds of inactivity the bubble is frozen, its displayed points and stage are held, motion stops, and it cannot occupy a winning seat or win the round. A new actual interaction resumes it. At120 seconds without activity it is removed from this game; the supporter archive remains.
- Each real like is1 point, each real gift coin is1 point, and a correct free quiz answer is10. No extra points for repeated answers to one question. Entry remains free, no prize or redemption is offered, and there are no spoken/written gift, like or follow requests.
- Stage thresholds use an OR condition: stage1 =1 coin or100 likes; stage2 =10 or500; stage3 =50 or1000; stage4 =100 or2500; stage5 =500 or5000; stage6 =1000 or10000; stage7 =2000 or20000; stage8 =5000 or50000. Points are displayed only in a reserved bottom rim inside the bubble, below the portrait image area.
- Stages change size, blade/rim geometry, light strength, rotation speed and ember particles. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. No points are taken from another user by a contact animation.
- Empty winning seats use clearly labelled SAMPLE portraits with rotating idle frames, not fake winners or fake viewer statistics.
- Every30 seconds at most one real visible person receives a short welcome in the selected Persian/English narrator language, while the LIVE bridge is connected. Spoken names keep letters only; digits and symbols are removed. The same person is not welcomed more than once in5 minutes. Samples/test participants are excluded. No visitor means no invented welcome.
- Dynamic welcomes reuse signed cached speech and the existing5000-character/day provider limit. Test welcome clips are pre-generated once in both languages. Mute, voice enable and host speech ducking are preserved.
- Private test tab adds stage cycling, freeze/resume and a welcome voice sample; it does not inject events into LIVE.
