# Free entertainment rules — version 63

- Profile studio: one answer (1–4) per participant per timed round. Correct answer: 10 points; incorrect answer: 0. Repeated answers, likes, gifts and follows add no points. Equal correct scores are shared, not resolved by spending.
- Football opinion poll: one free choice per participant per round. Each choice contributes 10 team points. Additional messages or payments do not buy votes.
- No monetary, gift or redemption prizes. Paid chance-card routes redirect to the free profile studio.
- No recurring spoken engagement requests, paid-entry rules, milestone promotions or follow CTAs. Card sound effects are retained.
- Fictional sample profiles and simulated poll results are labelled SAMPLE. Their values are not written to real ledgers.
- A one-time migration archives pre-change competitive snapshots privately and preserves the supporter archive. Existing timer duration, narrator language, sound controls and live retry schedule remain.
- An existing TikTok restriction is not removed by this software update. TikTok makes enforcement decisions; this is not a certification or guarantee of approval.
