Audio credits

The background loop is an original, gentle 88 BPM piano arrangement made for this game. Its instrument sound uses Alexander Holm’s Salamander Grand Piano V3 samples, distributed with attribution under CC BY 3.0. Samples were pitch-shifted, mixed, given room reverb and encoded into a new instrumental loop.

The recorded paper/card movement sounds are from Kenney — Casino Audio, CC0 1.0. Source draw/play files are also documented in the audio source manifest. Playback uses short variations in pitch and timing.

No paid music subscription or inference service is used.

Card-flip foley generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects, eleven_text_to_sound_v2, one 0.8-second effect. Playback is synchronized with the card turn.
