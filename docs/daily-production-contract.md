# Mandatory daily production contract

Active from episode 24, with `PROJECT_RULES.md` as the canonical owner policy.

1. Keep one ordinary GPU production start per Berlin day, from 07:30. The HF
   allowance resets at the service's own timestamp, not necessarily midnight.
2. Before choosing new content, ask the existing authenticated social monitor to
   refresh and read prior-post feedback. Save the attempt, evidence timestamp,
   available observations and one proposed measurable experiment. Feed that review
   to the story writer and record it in each newly authored plan's creative review.
   A pending episode keeps its exact signed plan and inputs.
3. Describe low/high views as observations. Same-age/platform comparisons,
   completion, watch time, shares per view and actual visual review matter.
   Missing metrics are recorded explicitly; never invent an explanation or promise
   virality. If analytics are unavailable, use a declared baseline quality test.
4. Read live included quota and inspect the exact saved input/audio/video signatures.
   Forecast only the missing work for the entire episode. Reuse the two speaker
   keyframes and all matching independent speaking takes.
5. For each provider-bound class, use the upper debit among the latest nine completed
   observations plus 20%. With fewer than three samples, use the published maximum.
   Retain 30 quota units and enough extra headroom for the full published maximum
   of the last outstanding call. Shared-account activity can inflate observed debits.
   This is a conservative forecast, **not a guaranteed runtime or final bill**.
6. If the whole forecast does not fit, save the pending plan and wait without a GPU
   call or consuming a production start. A direct future-episode render also needs
   a matching same-day review and admitted budget proof. Unbudgeted video previews
   are disabled for these daily episodes.
7. Continue checking actual quota and reserving the full provider maximum before
   **every** call. The forecast cannot override that guard or enable paid fallback.
   Do not shorten, loop or time-stretch material to pretend the 45 seconds are complete.
8. Confirm the complete image preview before speaking-video calls; retain quality
   gates and private TikTok/Instagram delivery receipts. Pillow 12.3.0 is pinned in
   daily production and the one-off completion flow to reproduce preview bytes.

Evidence lives in `renders/cinematic-series-cache/readiness/YYYY-MM-DD/` and is
archived with the daily workflow. `prior-post-review.json` records the analysis;
`episode-NNNN.json` binds quota admission to the exact plan hash. Completed cached
episodes require no new GPU budget. Existing `budgets/*.json` remain the debit ledger.
